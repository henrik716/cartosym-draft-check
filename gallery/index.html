Where the requirement text leaves a renderer a choice, or left one before it was tightened, each card draws the same small map with each reading. Part 1: vector styling

Symbolizer opacity: whole symbol or each part#

What the draft says
/req/core/opacity
  • An Encoding SHALL support defining an opacity Symbolizer property as a number between 0 (fully transparent) and 1 (fully opaque).
  • A Renderer SHALL support applying the resolved opacity property by multiplying it with the opacity of the fill, the stroke, the graphics of markers and the opacity channel of coverage cells.
/rec/core/opacity-together
  • A Renderer SHOULD apply the Symbolizer opacity to the fill, the stroke and the graphics of markers together, so that they do not show through each other where they overlap, either for each feature or for all features of a data layer with the same zOrder drawn at once.

With opacity: 0.5, is the symbol made translucent as a whole, or are fill and stroke made translucent separately? Separately, the stroke and fill overlap shows through. Does the opacity apply to the label?

As Jérôme answered on #162, the requirement says the opacity multiplies the opacity of the fill, the stroke, the marker graphics and the coverage cells, and a NOTE says it does not apply to labels. Casing beneath or above the stroke#

What the draft says
/req/casing/casing
  • An Encoding SHALL support defining a casing Stroke property as a StrokeStyling object.
  • A Renderer SHALL support drawing the casing of a stroke along the same geometry as the stroke, using the StrokeStyling specified by the casing property, beneath the main stroke.
  • A Renderer SHALL support drawing the casing so that it extends the width of the casing beyond each side of the main stroke, for a total width of the main stroke's width plus twice the casing's width.
  • A Renderer SHALL support drawing, within a single visualization pass, the casings of all features with the same zOrder beneath the main strokes of all those features, so that the casings of crossing and joining lines merge.
/req/casing/center
  • An Encoding SHALL support defining a center Stroke property as a StrokeStyling object.
  • A Renderer SHALL support drawing the center line of a stroke along the same geometry as the stroke, using the StrokeStyling specified by the center property, on top of the main stroke.
  • A Renderer SHALL support drawing, within a single visualization pass, the center lines of all features with the same zOrder on top of the main strokes of all those features, so that the main stroke of a joining line does not cut a center line.

A road with a 14 px yellow stroke, a red casing extending 4 px on each side at 60 % opacity (so what lies under it shows through) and a thin white centre line. Is the casing drawn beneath the stroke (an outline) or on top of it?

Units of dashPattern#

What the draft says
/req/dashes/dash-pattern
  • An Encoding SHALL support defining a dashPattern property for the stroke symbolizer.
  • A Renderer SHALL support dashing vector geometry based on a dashPattern property, whose lengths are in pixels unless qualified with another unit (<<graphical-units-of-measure,Graphical Units of Measure>>).
  • A Renderer SHALL support dashPattern lengths in pixels, points, millimeters, centimeters and inches.
/per/dashes/dash-pattern-units
  • A Renderer MAY support dashPattern lengths in real-world units (meters and feet).
  • A Renderer MAY support dashPattern lengths in percent of the stroke width, for a stroke whose width is in pixels, points, millimeters, centimeters or inches.
  • A Renderer MAY support dashPattern lengths in percent of the stroke width, for a stroke whose width is in meters or feet.

A 6 px wide line with dashPattern: [4, 2]. Are unqualified values pixels, or multiples of the stroke width?

Style (CartoSym-JSON)Edit in playground
{

    {
      "symbolizer": {
        "stroke": {

Pixels unless qualified, as Jérôme gave for label spacing and gradients, and as SE does for stroke-dasharray; MapLibre's line-dasharray is in multiples of the line width. Sources: SE 1.1, Stroke: stroke-dasharray lengths in the symbolizer's units of measure · GeoServer: stroke-dasharray in pixels · MapLibre line-dasharray: units in line widths

Example 8: what hillShading factor means#

What the draft says
/req/hill-shading/hill-shading
  • An Encoding SHALL support defining a hillShading Symbolizer property as an object including factor, sun, colorMap and opacityMap properties.
  • A Renderer SHALL support computing the terrain surface normal of each cell from the differences in elevation between neighbouring samples of the elevation coverage at the resolution being rendered, over the horizontal distance between those samples, in the same units as the elevation.
  • A Renderer SHALL support taking the elevation samples at about the resolution being rendered: the cells of the elevation coverage where a pixel of the map covers no more ground than a cell (for example 75 m pixels over 150 m cells), and otherwise elevations at about the size of a pixel, for example from overviews or levels of detail.
  • A Renderer SHALL support portraying an elevation coverage with a shade value computed for each cell as the dot product of the unit normal of the terrain surface and the unit vector pointing towards the sun, clamped to the range 0 to 1, where 0 is full shade (the sun at 90 degrees or more from the normal) and 1 is no shade (the sun in the direction of the normal).
  • A Renderer SHALL support, where the HillShading has no colorMap, multiplying the red, green and blue components of each cell by its shade value.
/req/hill-shading/factor
  • An Encoding SHALL support defining a factor HillShading property as a number.
  • A Renderer SHALL support multiplying the horizontal components of the terrain surface normal by the factor property before normalizing it, so that a factor of 1 shades the terrain as it is and a larger factor exaggerates the relief.
  • A Renderer SHALL support a factor of 0, its default value, as no hill shading, so that a hillShading of { } portrays the coverage as if hillShading were not set.
