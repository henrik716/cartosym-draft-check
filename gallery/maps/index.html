Gallery maps

Maps on the Rondane data (Kartverket N50, CC BY 4.0) that combine a requirement of OGC CartoSym Part 1 with land cover, relief and rivers as context; the context is not part of the style. They were the requirement figures of #192 until the vignettes replaced them, and are not in the document. Rendered by the draft renderer of cartosym-draft-check (python -m cartosym_draft.doc_examples).
