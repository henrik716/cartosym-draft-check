CartoSym 2.0 draft: every requirement in PR #133

Each requirement with its text, how it is checked, and links to the evidence: a gallery card for judgement calls, a sample render (below each class) for straightforward visual requirements, test cases for expressions, or a note for encodings. Samples show what the draft renderer draws; "Check" says what to look for. 134 requirements: 34 with a gallery card, 38 with a sample render, 66 with test cases, 11 checked as encodings, 2 partly and 1 not implemented. A requirement can be checked in more than one way.

text
  • An Encoding SHALL support individually storing and preserving the order of each StylingRule constituting a Style.
  • A Renderer SHALL support considering in order each individual StylingRule constituting a style.
  • The Symbolizer properties specified in a StylingRule appearing later in a Style's list of rules and whose Selector

/req/core/selector
text
  • An Encoding SHALL support defining an optional selector for a StylingRule as an Expression evaluating to a boolean value.
  • A Renderer SHALL support applying the Symbolizer of a StylingRule to a feature or coverage cell only if the rule has no selector or if its selector evaluates to true for that feature or coverage cell.

/req/core/nested
text
  • An Encoding SHALL support defining a StylingRule with an ordered list of nested StylingRules.
  • A Renderer SHALL support considering the nested rules of a StylingRule only if that parent rule is selected.
  • A Renderer SHALL support the Symbolizer of a selected nested rule inheriting from, and overriding, the Symbolizer properties of its parent rule.

text
  • An Encoding SHALL support defining a Symbolizer for a StylingRule, specifying any subset of the Symbolizer properties.

/req/cscss/rules
text
  • An Encoding SHALL support encoding a style as a sequence of styling rules, each consisting of zero or more selectors, an optional .name, symbolizer property assignments and nested rules enclosed within { }.
  • An Encoding SHALL support assigning a value to a member of an object property (using .) or to an element of an array property (using [ ]), overriding only that portion of the property.
  • A Renderer SHALL support symbolizer properties set further down in rules overriding those set by rules at the same level appearing earlier, and properties set in nested rules overriding those set in the parent rule.

text

  • A Renderer SHALL support combining multiple expression selectors of a rule with a logical AND, combining multiple data layer name selectors with a logical OR, and combining those two groups with a logical AND.

RequirementChecked byEvidence
/req/hill-shading/hill-shading
text
  • An Encoding SHALL support defining a hillShading Symbolizer property as an object including factor, sun, colorMap and opacityMap properties.
  • A Renderer SHALL support computing the terrain surface normal of each cell from the differences in elevation between neighbouring samples of the elevation coverage at the resolution being rendered, over the horizontal distance between those samples, in the same units as the elevation.
  • A Renderer SHALL support portraying an elevation coverage with a shade value computed for each cell as the dot product of the unit normal of the terrain surface and the unit vector pointing towards the sun, clamped to the range 0 to 1, where 0 is full shade (the sun at 90 degrees or more from the normal) and 1 is no shade (the sun in the direction of the normal).
  • A Renderer SHALL support, where the HillShading has no colorMap, multiplying the red, green and blue components of each cell by its shade value.
